The source definition of a bump supplies both nonzeroness and compact support. The formal statement includes both hypotheses, continuous differentiability and positive finite dimension, with the same gradient-range conclusion.

The proof-route adaptation is the included finite-dimensional variational proof of zero-section displacement rigidity. It discharges an ingredient needed by the gradient-graph argument without assuming a Floer-theoretic existence or rigidity result. This changes the supporting proof, not the advertised mathematical claim.

This record covers Theorem 1.1. It makes no completion claim about the other main results in the manuscript.
